---
Emreh is an all-in-one personal knowledge and memory system built to replace the fragmented collection of apps people use to manage their digital lives.

It brings together the capabilities of Obsidian, Notion, Anytype, AppFlowy, SiYuan, AFFiNE, Logseq, Tana, Capacities, Craft, Bear, Roam Research, Evernote, Raindrop, Karakeep, Linkwarden, Pocket, Readwise, Heptabase, RemNote, Day One, Diarium, Journey, Khoj, Mem, and more.

The core idea is simple: you should never feel like you are working with a database.

Import your existing data, sync it in both directions, connect your services, or let Emreh automatically fetch and organize information when you grant permission. It stays simple on the surface while powerful systems work underneath.

Its interface is built around speed, accessibility, and aesthetics, making your entire knowledge base feel natural and effortless to use.

At the center is your Emreh — an intelligent personal companion that understands and connects information across all your sources. It helps you discover relationships, retrieve forgotten information, and make sense of everything you have collected over time.

Emreh is not just a place to store information. It is a unified layer for your entire digital life.

And even a thousand years from now, you can return to Emreh and see not only what you saved, but how you lived.
